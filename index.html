Security
Checking…
Your schedule link is the key. Treat it like a password: don't post it publicly, and re-share a new one if it ever leaks.
Backups — restore if something gets deleted
A backup saves automatically as you make changes (at most every 30 min); the newest 20 are kept. Restoring backs up your current schedule first, so you can undo it.
